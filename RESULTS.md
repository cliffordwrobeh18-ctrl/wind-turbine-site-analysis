## Conclusion
In the ENGR 1102 course analysis, Concord, California had the highest estimated annual profit of the three evaluated locations. Distance to the electrical grid affected transmission losses and cost, while energy production had a major effect on overall profitability.

These are educational course-project estimates, not a commercial feasibility study.
